# FMLG ED chart layout (restated from FMLG_ED_Chart_FORMAT-MASTER.md, Oct 15 2026)

Authoritative source: `FMLG.REFERENCE.READONLY/03_FINANCIAL DISCOVERY/FMLG_ED_Chart_FORMAT-MASTER.md` and `.xlsx`. If this restatement and the master ever differ, the master governs.

1. Columns in order: Asset / liability · Titled to · Total value · Nonmarital portion · Marital value · Petitioner · Respondent. Optional columns (each party's FA figure, document value, notes) only to the right of column 7. A two- or three-column chart is retired.
2. Allocations are dollar values in the Petitioner and Respondent columns — never a P / R code column in their place. A 50/50 item shows half in each.
3. Titled to comes from the deed, account or statement — never from who uses the item. Values: Petitioner, Respondent, Joint, Joint (tenants by the entireties), Joint (lease), In possession, Not confirmed (no deed) / Not confirmed.
4. Assets first, grouped (real property; bank, investment and retirement; vehicles; business and personal property) with Total assets across columns 3–7; liabilities next (secured; unsecured) with Total liabilities.
5. Nonmarital items stay on the chart with total and nonmarital portion, $0 marital, out of the party columns.
6. Unvalued or undecided items stay on the chart ("Not valued", "Not determined", "Undecided"), are excluded from totals, and are named in a note as items that could change the equalizer. Reference-only figures carry an asterisk and a footnote.
7. Equalizer block last, always shown, arithmetic shown: net marital estate / net to each party; equal share target (50%); equalizing payment — [payor] to [payee]; net to each party after the payment.
8. Excel: every total, net, target and equalizer is a live formula; HTML and artifact compute the same way and match to the cent.
9. Sale: home and mortgage at a stated value, half the marital value to each party, note the value used and that the sale price controls. Every scenario keeps the same layout.
10. Petitioner / Respondent only; liabilities in parentheses; cents exact; unverified values labeled as stated on the FA or provided in session; letterhead on anything leaving the firm; no brand styling on anything court-bound.
